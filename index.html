Sponsored shortlist

Roles not yet applied to where Skilled Worker sponsorship is confirmed in the advert or the employing entity holds a licence. Earliest deadline first.

Confirmed in advert

—

The advert itself says it offers, welcomes or considers Skilled Worker sponsorship. Strongest evidence.

Licensed, not confirmed

—

The employing legal entity is on the Home Office register, but the advert is silent. Ask before investing time.

Licence unknown

—

The legal entity could not be matched to the register yet. Kept on the watchlist, never in the shortlist.
